AENEID TRAIL — How to play from this folder
===========================================

EASIEST WAY (recommended):
  1. Double-click  AeneidTrail.html
  2. It opens in your browser and the full game runs.
     No server needed. Works offline.

ALTERNATIVE:
  - Open index.html  (it will redirect to the single-file game)

MODULAR VERSION (for developers / editing):
  - Go into the  modular/  folder
  - Open  aeneid_game.html
  - Requires the css/, data/, and js/ subfolders to stay together

The single-file version (AeneidTrail.html) already contains
everything: full story, systems, random events, and endings.
